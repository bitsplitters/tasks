Team Tasks Manager is in **open beta**. Every feature — lists, reminders, webhooks, the lot — is unlocked and free, for every server, for the entire beta. No trial clock, no card, no greyed-out buttons.

## When the beta ends

When TTM leaves beta it becomes **freemium**. That word makes people nervous, so here's exactly what happens — no small print:

- **Nothing you've built is ever deleted or hidden.** Your lists, tasks and reminders stay exactly where they are.
- **A free tier stays free, forever.** A small team can keep using TTM without paying a cent.
- **If your server has outgrown the free limits**, you simply can't add *more* beyond them until you upgrade — everything already there stays.
- **What you build during the beta stays.** The free limits apply only to what you add *next*: the lists, tasks and reminders you created during the beta keep working even if they sit above the thresholds: they stay in the channel, their reminders keep arriving and their tasks can still be checked — see *What a beta list keeps* below for what needs one of your three places. You were the ones who showed up early — you don't get to be the ones penalised for it.

**What a beta list keeps.** On the free tier you work on three lists at a time: a list takes one of the three places the first time someone uses it after the beta, and keeps it. A list created during the beta that has no place stays in the channel exactly as it is: its reminders keep arriving, its tasks can still be checked off — without it taking a place — and it still downloads. Its webhooks fire only once it has a place, and everything else — bulk update, Clean, Reopen all, owners, tags, reminders — needs one, and takes it if one is free. To free a place, use **Seal list** or **Delete list** on a list you're done with. Recurring reminders set up during the beta keep firing too, and stay editable: you can move them or lower their repetitions, not raise them.

## The free tier, after beta

Three active lists, twenty tasks each, ten reminders waiting at a time — single ones, since recurring reminders are Premium — and no webhooks: enough for a small team to keep using TTM without ever paying.
